# NOTICE

## No third-party assets

This repo contains no third-party images, fonts, libraries, or data.
Styling uses system fonts only. Browser Web Speech APIs are built into
the browser and are not redistributed here.

## Seed data is fictional

All seed board entries use obviously fictional company names (for
example "متجر تجريبي") and invented cases. They exist only to demonstrate
the board, the score display, and the company-reply slot, and they are
labeled as demo data in the UI. No real company is named anywhere in
this repo, and no real person's complaint is included.

## Legal status of the letter draft

The letter template is a writing aid, not legal advice and not a legal
filing. The user reviews the draft, corrects it, and sends it in their
own name, with their own facts, through the channel they choose. No
consumer-protection-law article number or quoted provision appears in
this repo: law grounding is a placeholder until provisions are verified
during the build (see NEW_WORK.md).
